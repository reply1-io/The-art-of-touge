What the parts work out to

The sound is synthesized live from these numbers: pressure pulses timed by the crank, down header and pipe delay lines set by length and gas temperature, through a muffler model set by its type and volume. Gas temperatures assumed: 800 °C in the headers, 450 °C down the pipe.
