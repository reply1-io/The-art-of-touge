Why it sounds like a six

Each cylinder fires every 120° of crank, so the main pitch is rpm ÷ 20 (40 Hz at idle, 325 Hz at the limiter). Small differences between cylinders add the half-order rumble a perfectly even buzz would lack.

On and off the gas

Under load the pulses hit harder and the rasp opens up. Lift above idle and the fuel is cut on overrun, so the exhaust goes soft and the odd pop comes through.

Tuning it

Each engine is a profile in m20-dsp.js: firing order, crank angles, banks, pipe lengths, muffler voicing and cylinder imbalance. Street and Race open the muffler, add rasp and rumble, and give the idle a heavy lope.
