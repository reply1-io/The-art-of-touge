Why it sounds like a six

Each cylinder fires every 120° of crank, so the main pitch is rpm ÷ 20 (40 Hz at idle, 325 Hz at the limiter). Small differences between cylinders add the half-order rumble a perfectly even buzz would lack.

On and off the gas

Under load the pulses hit harder and the rasp opens up. Lift above idle and the fuel is cut, as Motronic does on overrun, so the exhaust goes soft and the odd pop comes through.

Tuning it

Pipe length, manifold runner lengths, muffler cutoff and the cylinder imbalance are all numbers in m20-worklet.js. The Sport setting opens the muffler and adds rasp.
