What's real

From 1,000 to 2,200 rpm you hear your car with a pitch correction of a few percent at most. That's the green arc on the tach.

What's stretched

Above 2,200 rpm the closest slices are pitched up and a synthesized layer firing three times per revolution fills in the body. The higher you go, the less it sounds like your car.

To get the howl

Record steady holds at 3,000, 4,000, 5,000 and 6,000 rpm, plus a pull in 2nd gear with a phone taped near the exhaust. Run the same script on the clip and the green arc grows to cover it.
